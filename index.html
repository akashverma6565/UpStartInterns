Full-stack quiz platform

The clock doesn't lie, and neither does the score.

QuizMaster runs timed quizzes where the countdown is real and the grading happens on the server. Students never see a correct answer before they submit — because it never leaves the database until they do.

What's actually in the app

Four things that came out of building this to a real spec, not a tutorial.

A

Role-based access

Students take quizzes. Admins build and manage them. Signing in routes each person to the right view based on their role.

B

Live countdown, real auto-submit

Every quiz runs a per-question or per-quiz timer. When it hits zero, whatever's answered gets submitted automatically — no silent expiry, no lost attempt.

C

Server-side scoring

The correct answer is stripped out before a question is ever sent to the browser. Grading happens after submission, against the stored key.

D

Aggregated leaderboard

Scores roll up into a leaderboard per quiz, so students can see where an attempt landed relative to everyone else who took it.

From login to leaderboard

  1. 1

    Sign in

    The app checks the account's role and sends students and admins to different parts of the app.

  2. 2

    Start the quiz

    Questions load without their answers attached, and the countdown starts the moment the first one renders.

  3. 3

    Submit, or get auto-submitted

    Answers post to the server, which checks them against the stored key. Run out the clock and the current answers go in for you.

  4. 4

    Check the leaderboard

    The score aggregates into the quiz's leaderboard right after grading, no refresh required.

The rule this app is built around

An answer never reaches the browser before the quiz is submitted.

It's enforced at the query level with .select('-answer') on every question document sent to a student, not by hiding the field in the UI. That means opening dev tools mid-quiz doesn't help — the correct option simply isn't part of the payload yet.
